# Path to Becoming a Recognized Expert

1. **Pick one niche** and say no to most other topics. (See [voice-agent strategy](../research/VOICE_AGENTS_STRATEGY.md).)
2. **Read the core literature** — 30–50 papers. Keep notes in `knowledge/reading_notes/`.
3. **Reproduce 2–3 key results** before claiming anything new.
4. **Release useful artifacts** — a dataset, a benchmark harness, a model. Artifacts get cited more than opinions.
5. **Publish** — start with workshops, then main conferences.
6. **Teach in public** — YouTube (English + Urdu), blog posts, talks. Explain each paper you publish.
7. **Collaborate** — reach out to authors of papers you build on; a PhD or research lab speeds this up.
8. **Track your impact** — citations, dataset downloads, GitHub stars, invited talks.
